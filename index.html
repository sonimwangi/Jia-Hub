About JIA HUB

A research studio built for builders who move fast.

JIA HUB is a technology research and consultancy studio focused on the systems shaping the next generation of the internet.

Our work spans Web3, Bitcoin, AI, and emerging digital infrastructure. We combine research with practical ecosystem experience to help founders and teams make better decisions.

We understand that building is only one part of the equation. You also need to understand the market, the community, the ecosystem, and the people you are building for.

That is where we come in. We bring together research, strategy, content, community, and on-the-ground experience to turn complex ideas into clear opportunities.
